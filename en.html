FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does trademark registration take?

In Türkiye, without opposition, the process is usually completed within 6–8 months. Abroad, timing varies by country.

How long is a trademark registration valid?

A registration is valid for 10 years from the filing date and can be renewed every 10 years.

Is a search required before filing?

It is not mandatory, but strongly recommended to reduce the risk of refusal and opposition. We do the preliminary search free of charge.

Is my trademark protected in other countries automatically?

No. Trademark protection is territorial; you need a registration in each country or region where you want protection.

Do I need a registered trademark to sell on Amazon?

Amazon Brand Registry requires a registered or pending trademark in the relevant country.

Can I get protection in several countries with one application?

Yes. Under the Madrid Protocol, you can designate member countries in a single application.
