US

United StatesUSPTO

Requires use declarations and detailed goods/services descriptions. Foreign-domiciled applicants must be represented by a US-licensed attorney; we work with partner attorneys.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
EU

European UnionEUIPO

One application, one registration, protection in all 27 EU member states.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
GB

United KingdomUKIPO

Since Brexit, an EU trademark no longer covers the UK; a separate filing is needed.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
CN

ChinaCNIPA

First-to-file system. Filing early, before manufacturing or sales, is critical.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
GCC

UAE & Gulf StatesNational offices

Protection in the Gulf is usually obtained through separate filings in each country.

Filing route: Country-by-country
JP

JapanJPO

A strong and predictable registration system for brands entering Asia.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
CA

CanadaCIPO

Best planned together with the US as part of a North American strategy.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
RU

RussiaRospatent

Protection for companies exporting to the region, nationally or internationally.

Filing route: Direct or Madrid
AFR

AfricaARIPO · OAPI

Regional systems covering many African countries with a single filing.

About Us

Your trusted partner in intellectual property

Since 2010, International Trademark Patent Center Inc. has been securing businesses' trademark, patent and design rights in Türkiye and around the world.

With our offices in Çekmeköy and Şişli in Istanbul, 6 offices across Anatolia and 26 partner offices abroad, we handle the whole process under one roof, from search to registration and from oppositions to portfolio management.

Our approach is simple: tell you your chances as they are, never miss a deadline, and give every client a single point of contact.
